About Household Expiration Tracker

Household Expiration Tracker is a browser-based application designed to help users organize and monitor the expiration dates of their household items.

Users can record their items, view their expiration status, manage their inventory, and keep track of items that have already been consumed or discarded.

Our Purpose

The application aims to make it easier for households to keep track of items that may expire or become unusable. Instead of relying on memory, users can use the tracker to quickly check which items need attention.

What Can You Track?

Food

Monitor expiration dates of food and other consumable household items.

Medicine

Keep track of medicines and their expiration dates.

Personal Care

Track toiletries, skincare products, and other personal care items.

Household Supplies

Monitor cleaning supplies, pet supplies, emergency supplies, and other household items.

How It Works

1. Add an Item

Enter the item's name, category, quantity, and expiration date.

2. Monitor Your Inventory

View your saved items and check their current expiration status.

3. Take Action

When an item needs attention, you can edit, delete, or mark it as done.

4. View Item History

Items marked as deleted, consumed, or discarded are moved to your item history.

Expiration Statuses

Items are automatically grouped based on the number of days remaining before their expiration date.

Good

31+ days remaining

Soon

16–30 days remaining

Urgent

8–15 days remaining

Critical

1–7 days remaining

Expired

0 or fewer days remaining

Benefits

  • Helps keep household items organized.
  • Makes expiration dates easier to monitor.
  • Helps users identify items that need attention.
  • Provides a record of consumed or discarded items.
