🚨 72-Hour Emergency Go-Bag Guide

During a fire, flood, or earthquake, you have seconds to evacuate. Keep these critical items tracked and rotated every 6 months.

🍞 Food & Water Supplies

  • 4–5 liters of potable drinking water per person
  • Ready-to-eat canned goods (tuna, corned beef, beans)
  • High-calorie energy bars, crackers, and biscuits
  • Manual can opener and durable plastic utensils

💊 Medical & Sanitation

  • Prescription medications (7-day emergency supply)
  • First-aid kit (gauze, antiseptic, burn ointments)
  • Water purification tablets or drops
  • N95 face masks (crucial for fire smoke & debris)

🔦 Survival & Documents

  • Heavy-duty LED flashlight & extra fresh batteries
  • Whistle (to signal search and rescue teams)
  • Sealed waterproof pouch with IDs, deeds, and cash
  • Power bank, USB cable, and hand-crank emergency radio
